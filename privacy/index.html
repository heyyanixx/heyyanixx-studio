Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 Nov 2026

HEYYANIXX Studio ("we", "us") makes websites for local businesses. This page explains, in simple words, what information we get from you and what we do with it.

What we collect

We only get what you send us yourself, by email or Instagram message:

  • Your name and how to reach you (email or Instagram)
  • Your business details (name, address, timings, services, prices)
  • Content for your website (text, photos, logo)

How we use it

  • To talk to you about your project
  • To build, launch and update your website
  • Information you ask us to put on your website (like your shop address or timings) will be public on that website

What we don't do

  • We never sell your information.
  • We don't share it with anyone, except services needed to make your website work (for example a domain or hosting provider), and only what they need.

This website

This website has no sign-up and no ads. We don't use tracking cookies or analytics tools. The help chat on the home page gives ready-made answers inside your browser only: what you type in it is not sent, saved or shared anywhere. Like any website, our web host may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

How long we keep it

We keep your project details while we work together and for as long as needed to support your website. Email us anytime to see, correct or delete your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.

This policy follows the laws of India.
